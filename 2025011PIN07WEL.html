Onboarding Welcome Screens.

I conceptualized, designed and worked with our back-end developers to implement a set of Onboarding/Welcome screens to introduce features of the Online newspaper.

First prototypes

We wanted to avoid overwhelming readers with too much information and focus on a brief list of features that readers had indicated the most interest in. Initial designs offered more interactive animations.

Final prototypes

The final prototype lacked the initial visual dazzle of the interactive animations but provided a clearer path and better performance.
